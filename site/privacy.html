Privacy notice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to your personal data when you join the Vulnpilot waitlist on this website. It does not cover the Vulnpilot command-line tool, whose data handling is described in its README.

Who is responsible

The controller is the operator of Vulnpilot. For anything about your data, email quavia.business@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • your email address;
  • your company size, if you choose to give it;
  • how you found the page (for example cli if you followed the link printed by the Vulnpilot CLI, or direct);
  • the date and time you signed up;
  • that you ticked the consent box.

Giving this data is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot add you to the waitlist.

Why we use it

To send you announcements about Vulnpilot, such as when continuous monitoring and report drafts become available to try. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising or use it for automated decision-making.

Legal basis

Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Where it is stored

Signups are stored in a database run by Supabase, our processor, in its EU region (Frankfurt, Germany). The website itself is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the page and may keep them briefly in server logs, under its data processing terms.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, and at the latest 12 months after the paid Vulnpilot product launches. Then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent, by emailing quavia.business@gmail.com. Every email we send will also include a way to unsubscribe. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

No tracking, no cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts or third-party scripts.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the date above. If a change affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email first.
