Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to your personal data when you use this website: when you join the Vulnpilot waitlist, and when you use the browser scanner at /scan. It does not cover the Vulnpilot command-line tool, whose data handling is described in its README.

Who is responsible

The controller is the operator of Vulnpilot. For anything about your data, email quavia.business@gmail.com.

Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel, which, like any web host, processes your IP address and browser details to deliver the pages and files you request, including the copy of the CISA catalog of known exploited vulnerabilities that the browser scanner loads from /api/kev, and may keep them briefly in server logs, under its data processing terms.

The waitlist

What we collect

  • your email address;
  • your company size, if you choose to give it;
  • how you found the page (for example cli if you followed the link printed by the Vulnpilot CLI, or direct);
  • the date and time you signed up;
  • that you ticked the consent box.

Giving this data is voluntary. Without an email address we cannot add you to the waitlist.

Why we use it

To send you announcements about Vulnpilot, such as when continuous monitoring and report drafts become available to try. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising or use it for automated decision-making.

Legal basis

Your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time; this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Where it is stored

Signups are stored in a database run by Supabase, our processor, in its EU region (Frankfurt, Germany).

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent, and at the latest 12 months after the paid Vulnpilot product launches. Then we delete it.

The browser scanner

The scanner at /scan reads the lockfile or SBOM you choose inside your browser tab. The file is not uploaded: not to Vercel, not to Supabase, not to us. We never receive the file, its contents or your results, and nothing is stored in your browser. Closing the tab discards them.

Only when you press Scan does your browser send requests, directly and not through us, to two public vulnerability services:

  • OSV.dev, an open vulnerability database run by Google: the names and versions of the packages in your file, including private or internal package names if the file lists them, and then the IDs of the advisories it returns;
  • FIRST, which publishes EPSS scores: the CVE IDs found.

Before you press Scan, the page lists what will be sent and lets you view the exact request. As with any web request, both services also receive your IP address and browser details. They are independent services that handle this data under their own terms and privacy policies, and may process it outside the EU.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing the scan you ask for (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Nothing is sent until you press Scan, and the command-line tool does the same scan without this website.

The scanner sends us no telemetry: we do not learn that you used it, which file you scanned or what it found.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent, by emailing quavia.business@gmail.com. Every email we send will also include a way to unsubscribe. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. For the browser scanner we hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to provide, correct or delete; to exercise your rights with OSV.dev or FIRST, contact them.

No tracking, no cookies

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and uses no analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts or third-party scripts. Apart from Vercel, the only services your browser contacts are Supabase, when you submit the waitlist form, and OSV.dev and FIRST, when you press Scan on /scan.

Changes

If this notice changes, we will update the date above. If a change affects how we use your data, we will tell you by email first.
