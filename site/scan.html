Scan

Scan a lockfile in this tab

The same code as npx vulnpilot, compiled for the browser. Your file is read here and never uploaded. Nothing leaves this tab until you press Scan, and then only what is listed before the button.

Input

Choose a file

Drop a lockfile or CycloneDX JSON SBOM here, choose one, or paste it.

package-lock.json, pnpm-lock.yaml, yarn.lock or CycloneDX JSON, up to 32 MB. Read by a worker in this tab that has no network access.

    CRA

    CRA screen

    Three questions, the CLI’s rules. The verdict updates as you answer and goes into the report. Answers stay in this tab.

    Is the product made available on the EU market (sold, distributed or offered to users in the EU)?
    Is it supplied as part of a commercial activity (sold, licensed, monetised, or with paid support/services)?
    What kind of product is it?

    Result

    Verdict
    UNCLEAR
    Rule
    insufficient-information

    The answers do not settle it yet: unknown whether you offer the product in the EU, whether it is part of a commercial activity, what kind of product it is. If you sell or distribute downloadable software or firmware to EU users as part of a business, the CRA most likely applies.

    Key dates

    • 11 September 2026 (in force): Vulnerability and incident reporting obligations apply: early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware of an actively exploited vulnerability or severe incident.
    • 11 December 2027: Full obligations apply, including essential cybersecurity requirements, conformity assessment and CE marking.

    Not legal advice. This is an automated preliminary screen based on three answers.

    As flags: --eu-distribution unknown --commercial unknown --product-type unknown
