Free, open-source CLI · MIT licensed

Know your CRA exposure and which dependencies are actively exploited.

Vulnpilot scans your lockfile or SBOM, flags vulnerabilities listed in CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, ranks the rest by EPSS and CVSS, and runs a short EU Cyber Resilience Act applicability screen. One command, no account.

$ npx vulnpilot

Requires Node.js 26 or later. Run it in a project directory.

What the free CLI does

Everything below ships in the current npm package. It reads only your lockfile or SBOM; no source code leaves your machine.

  • Inputs

    package-lock.json (npm), pnpm-lock.yaml, yarn.lock (Classic and Berry), and CycloneDX JSON SBOMs via --sbom for firmware and non-npm projects.

  • Data sources

    Known vulnerabilities from OSV.dev, the "actively exploited" flag from CISA KEV, and exploit likelihood from FIRST EPSS. Issues are ranked KEV first, then EPSS, then CVSS.

  • CRA screen

    Three questions (EU market, commercial activity, product type) give a rule-based first pass: likely applies, likely out of scope, or unclear, with the reason and the key dates.

  • JSON output and CI exit codes

    --json prints a versioned, schema-validated report. Exit codes 0/1/2 and --fail-on critical|high|kev let you gate a pipeline.

  • Offline cache

    Responses are cached for 24 hours. --offline scans from the cache only and reports anything that is missing.

Why now

The Cyber Resilience Act has dates

The EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847) covers products with digital elements made available on the EU market, including software. Two dates matter most.

  1. 11 September 2026 In force

    Reporting obligations apply

    Manufacturers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents: an early warning within 24 hours of becoming aware, a notification within 72 hours, and a final report once a fix or mitigation is available. This also covers products already on the market.

  2. 11 December 2027

    Full obligations and CE marking

    Essential cybersecurity requirements, vulnerability handling, conformity assessment and CE marking apply to products placed on the EU market.

Not legal advice. Vulnerability data and the CRA screen are an automated first pass. Whether and how the CRA applies to you depends on details a short questionnaire cannot capture; check with a qualified adviser.
